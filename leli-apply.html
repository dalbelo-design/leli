What Happens Next

Raising your hand costs nothing — and commits you to nothing.

Expressing interest just means we know how to find you. When a program opens in your area, we'll reach out, tell you what it involves, and you decide from there. In the meantime, you can learn more about what consultants do and what LELI commits to every consultant.

What consultants do See the curriculum

LELI

The Lived Experience Leadership Institute prepares people with lived experience to work as professional consultants — shaping the policy, implementation, and evaluation of the systems they've navigated.

Institutionalizing the preparation and refinement of lived experience into living expertise.
